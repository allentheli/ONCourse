References

Sources for every pathway

Links open the citation on PubMed. Schedules are also cross-checked against structured protocol references (HemOnc.org, eviQ) and current FDA labeling. Each pathway lists when it was last reviewed and by whom. "Physician sign-off pending" means the source check, which is AI-assisted, is complete and the founder's clinical sign-off is in progress.

Dosing and schedules can differ between a trial protocol, the FDA label, and institutional practice. ONCourse follows the published protocol and does not show doses. Confirm cycles, intervals, and doses against your institution's protocol before treating.

ONCourse is an educational aid for conversations between patients and their care teams. It is not medical advice and does not replace your clinic's appointment schedule. See the disclaimer and terms of use.
