# Treatment Roadmap — Regimen Verification Log
Verified August 31, 2026, against primary publications, trial protocols, and structured regimen references. Items marked **CORRECTED** were changed in this version. Between-phase intervals are protocol windows where the protocol specifies one; otherwise they are typical-practice defaults for you to adjust per patient.

## Corrections made in this version
1. **KEYNOTE-522** — patient text now states carboplatin may be given weekly or once per 3-week cycle (trial allowed AUC 1.5 weekly or AUC 5 q3w; OncLive/protocol summaries).
2. **RAPIDO** — added the ~2-week gap between short-course RT and CAPOX (protocol: chemotherapy commenced 11–18 days after the last RT day; NCT01558921 protocol).
3. **OPRA** — restaging window corrected to ~8 weeks after TNT completion (protocol: 8 ± 4 weeks; JCO/PMC secondary analyses).
4. **Colon stage III surveillance** — CT interval corrected from yearly to every 6–12 months (NCCN-consistent).

## Breast
- **KEYNOTE-522** (verified: OncLive trial summary; Annals of Oncology OS abstract). Pembro 200 mg q3w ×4 + weekly paclitaxel 80 + carboplatin (AUC 5 q3w or AUC 1.5 weekly) ×4 cycles → pembro + doxorubicin 60 (or epirubicin 90) + cyclophosphamide 600 q3w ×4 → surgery → adjuvant pembro ×9. Surgery window: 3–6 weeks after last neoadjuvant dose (app default 4). Adjuvant capecitabine (CREATE-X) / olaparib (OlympiA) are guideline extrapolations flagged "may be recommended" — not part of KN-522.
- **DESTINY-Breast11** (verified: Annals of Oncology primary publication). T-DXd 5.4 q3w ×4 → THP ×4 (weekly paclitaxel + trastuzumab + pertuzumab q3w) → surgery → adjuvant per SOC: pCR → trastuzumab ± pertuzumab to complete 1 year; residual → T-DM1 ×14 per protocol, with T-DXd ×14 now supported by DESTINY-Breast05 (FDA-approved 2026). App shows T-DXd ×14 with T-DM1 alternative.
- **DESTINY-Breast05** (verified: NEJM 2026;394:845; ESMO LBA1). T-DXd 5.4 vs T-DM1 3.6, q3w ×14 cycles; concurrent RT and endocrine allowed.
- **TCHP → HP** (standard-reference; NeoSphere/TRAIN-2/KATHERINE). TCHP q3w ×6; adjuvant HP ×11 to complete ~1 year of HER2 therapy; residual → T-DXd or T-DM1 ×14.
- **ddAC → weekly paclitaxel** (standard-reference; CALGB 9741 / ECOG 1199). AC q2w ×4 with growth factor → paclitaxel weekly ×12.
- **HR+ high-risk** (standard-reference; monarchE / NATALEE). Abemaciclib 2 years or ribociclib 3 years + endocrine 5–10 years.

## GI
- **PRODIGE 23** (verified: JCO abstracts, ASCO Daily News). mFOLFIRINOX (ox 85 / LV 400 / iri 180 / 5-FU 2400 over 46 h, no bolus) q2w ×6 → CRT 50 Gy/25 fx + capecitabine on RT days (5 wks) → surgery → adjuvant 3 months: mFOLFOX6 ×6 or capecitabine ×4. Pre-surgery interval ~6–8 weeks (typical practice; NCCN allows 5–12).
- **RAPIDO** (verified: Lancet Oncology; trial protocol). SCRT 5×5 Gy (≤8 days) → **11–18-day break (CORRECTED)** → CAPOX ×6 (cape 1000 BID d1–14 + ox 130 q3w) or FOLFOX4 ×9 → TME 2–4 weeks after last chemo. No adjuvant therapy.
- **OPRA** (verified: PMC long-term publication; JCO commentary). CRT 45 Gy/25 fx + boost to 50–56 Gy with capecitabine or 5-FU → consolidation FOLFOX q2w ×8 or CAPEOX q3w ×5 → **restage 8 ± 4 weeks after TNT (CORRECTED)** → cCR/near-cCR → watch-and-wait (exam/scope/MRI q3–4 months ×2 y); incomplete → TME.
- **FLOT ± durvalumab** (verified: eviQ FLOT protocol; MATTERHORN summaries + cost-effectiveness paper describing schedule). FLOT q2w ×4 pre and ×4 post; durvalumab 1500 q4w ×2 pre, ×2 post, then ×10 alone. Surgery aimed ~4 weeks after last preop dose (window 4–8 in MATTERHORN); adjuvant resumes 6–12 weeks post-op (app default 7, text "6 to 8" — within window).
- **CROSS → CheckMate 577** (standard-reference; NEJM CROSS; NEJM CM577). Weekly carbo AUC2 + paclitaxel 50 ×5 + 41.4 Gy/23 fx; surgery ~6–8 weeks after; residual disease → nivolumab 240 q2w ×16 weeks then 480 q4w to complete 1 year, starting 4–16 weeks post-op.
- **Colon stage III CAPOX** (standard-reference; IDEA). CAPOX ×4 (3 mo) most; ×8 (6 mo) high-risk; FOLFOX ×6/×12 alternative. **Surveillance CT every 6–12 months (CORRECTED)**; CEA q3–6 months; colonoscopy at 1 year.
- **PRODIGE 24** (standard-reference; NEJM). Adjuvant mFOLFIRINOX q2w ×12 starting within 3–12 weeks of surgery. Note: adjuvant mFOLFIRINOX uses irinotecan 150 (dose detail not shown on the patient page).

## Lung
- **KEYNOTE-671** (standard-reference; NEJM). Cisplatin + pemetrexed (or gemcitabine d1, d8) + pembro q3w ×4 → surgery within 20 weeks of first dose → adjuvant pembro ×13 starting 4–12 weeks post-op.
- **CheckMate 816** (standard-reference; NEJM). Nivo 360 + platinum doublet q3w ×3 → surgery within 6 weeks. Post-op "further treatment to be discussed" reflects trial design (no mandated adjuvant).
- **CheckMate 77T** (standard-reference; NEJM). Nivo + chemo q3w ×4 → surgery → nivo 480 q4w up to 1 year (×13).
- **PACIFIC** (standard-reference; NEJM + current label). Concurrent CRT ~6 weeks → durvalumab starting 1–42 days after, q2w or q4w up to 12 months.
- **ADAURA** (standard-reference; NEJM). Osimertinib 80 daily ×3 years; optional adjuvant cisplatin doublet ×4 first.
- **ALINA** (standard-reference; NEJM). Alectinib 600 BID ×2 years instead of chemotherapy.
- **LAURA** (standard-reference; NEJM). CRT → osimertinib daily until progression, starting within 6 weeks.
- **ADRIATIC** (standard-reference; NEJM). Platinum-etoposide ×4 + concurrent thoracic RT (45 Gy BID over 3 weeks or 60–66 Gy daily); optional PCI; durvalumab 1500 q4w up to 24 months, starting 1–42 days after CRT.

## Additions verified August 31, 2026 (v0.3)

### Breast (regrouped by receptor status)
- **APT** (standard-reference; Tolaney NEJM 2015). Weekly paclitaxel 80 + trastuzumab ×12, then trastuzumab q3w to complete 1 year (13 further doses). Stage I, node-negative, ≤3 cm.
- **TC ×4** (standard-reference; US Oncology 9735). Docetaxel 75 + cyclophosphamide 600 q3w ×4.
- **Endocrine-only** (guideline; TAILORx / ATLAS). 5–10 years of tamoxifen or an aromatase inhibitor; ovarian suppression for selected premenopausal women.

### GU
- **NIAGARA** (verified: NEJM 2024;391:1773; protocol NCT03732677). Durvalumab 1500 q3w + gemcitabine 1000 d1/d8 + cisplatin 70 d1 ×4 → radical cystectomy → durvalumab 1500 q4w ×8. FDA-approved 2025.
- **CheckMate 274** (verified: JCO 2024 expanded analysis; Ann Oncol 2025 5-year). Adjuvant nivolumab 240 q2w for ≤1 year, started within 120 days of surgery; the app shows q4w ×13 (label-permitted 480 mg schedule) with pembrolizumab (AMBASSADOR) noted as an alternative. Neoadjuvant GC ×4 / ddMVAC per SWOG 8710 and standard practice.
- **KEYNOTE-564** (verified: NEJM 2024 OS publication). Pembrolizumab 200 q3w up to 17 cycles (~1 year), within 12 weeks of nephrectomy.
- **Bladder-preserving trimodality** (standard-reference; BC2001). Maximal TURBT → chemoradiation ~6–7 weeks (cisplatin, or 5-FU + mitomycin) → cystoscopic surveillance.
- **Kidney surveillance** (NCCN). Partial or radical nephrectomy → scans every 6–12 months.
- **Prostate, high risk** (standard-reference; EORTC 22961, STAMPEDE 2022). ADT 2–3 years with radiation; abiraterone + prednisone 2 years for very-high-risk disease. Radiation course length left blank by design (5–39 fractions by technique).
- **Prostate, intermediate risk** (standard-reference; RTOG 94-08). ADT 4–6 months with radiation.
- **Prostatectomy → PSA surveillance** (standard-reference; ProtecT 2023, ARTISTIC 2020). Early salvage radiation if PSA rises.
- **Active surveillance** (standard-reference; ProtecT). PSA every 6 months; MRI and biopsy at intervals.

### Design change affecting all regimens
- Radiation steps that vary by technique (adjuvant breast, post-lung-surgery, prostate, salvage) now default to a blank length; protocol-defined chemoradiation courses (CROSS, PRODIGE 23, OPRA, PACIFIC, LAURA, ADRIATIC, trimodality bladder, RAPIDO short course) keep their trial lengths.

## Added September 1, 2026 (v0.3.1)

Cisplatin-ineligible MIBC: neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin 1.25 mg/kg d1, d8 + pembrolizumab 200 d1 q3w ×3 → radical cystectomy + PLND → adjuvant EV + pembrolizumab ×6, then pembrolizumab alone ×8 (14 adjuvant pembrolizumab cycles; 17 total). ## Added September 1, 2026 (v0.4.1)
- **IMvigor011** (verified: NEJM 2025, NEJMoa2511885; FDA approval May 15, 2026 with Signatera CDx). After radical cystectomy, serial ctDNA testing begins ≥6 weeks post-op (surveillance window 6–24 weeks) for up to 1 year; ctDNA-positive → atezolizumab 1680 mg IV q4w (or subcutaneous) for up to 12 cycles / 1 year; ctDNA-negative → continued surveillance. IMvigor011: DFS HR 0.64, OS HR 0.59. Neoadjuvant cisplatin chemotherapy is shown as an optional first step because most trial patients had received it.

## Added September 1, 2026 (v0.5): head and neck, melanoma, GI additions
- **ATOMIC** (verified: NEJM 2026; FDA priority review, decision expected Oct 9, 2026; NCCN category 2A preferred). Resected stage III dMMR/MSI-H colon: mFOLFOX6 ×12 (q2w) + atezolizumab 840 mg q2w ×12 → atezolizumab ×13 alone (12 months total). 3-year DFS 86% vs 76%, HR 0.50. Labeled in the app as NCCN-listed with the FDA decision pending.

- **Definitive chemoradiation, HNSCC** (standard-reference; MACH-NC; Noronha JCO 2018). Cisplatin 100 mg/m² q3w ×3 (or 40 mg/m² weekly) with 70 Gy/35 fx; PET/CT at ~12 weeks; neck dissection for residual nodal disease.
- **Post-operative RT ± cisplatin** (standard-reference; RTOG 9501, EORTC 22931). Cisplatin added for positive margins or extranodal extension; RT to start within 6 weeks of surgery.
- **KEYNOTE-689** (verified: FDA approval June 12, 2025; ClinicalTrials.gov protocol). PD-L1 CPS ≥1: pembrolizumab 200 q3w ×2 → surgery within 6 weeks → pembrolizumab ×3 with RT ± cisplatin → pembrolizumab ×12 (15 adjuvant, 17 total). EFS 59.7 vs 29.6 months in CPS ≥1.
- **Nasopharyngeal induction** (standard-reference; Zhang NEJM 2019). Gemcitabine 1000 d1,8 + cisplatin 80 d1 q3w ×3 → cisplatin 100 q3w ×3 with RT.
- **NADINA** (verified: NEJM 2024; ASCO Post; NCCN-listed, no FDA label). Ipilimumab 80 mg + nivolumab 240 mg q3w ×2 → therapeutic lymph node dissection at ~week 6 → major pathologic response (≤10% viable, 59%): no adjuvant; otherwise nivolumab 480 q4w ×11 or dabrafenib + trametinib 46 weeks if BRAF-mutant.
- **SWOG S1801** (standard-reference; NEJM 2023). Pembrolizumab 200 q3w ×3 → surgery → ×15 (18 total).
- **Adjuvant immunotherapy** (standard-reference; KEYNOTE-054, KEYNOTE-716, CheckMate 238). Pembrolizumab q3w ×17 or nivolumab q4w ×12 for one year.
- **COMBI-AD** (standard-reference; NEJM 2017). Dabrafenib 150 mg BID + trametinib 2 mg daily for 12 months, BRAF V600.

## Additions and changes verified September 1, 2026 (v0.8.0)

Sources retrieved from PubMed this session are marked **(fetched)**; items marked
standard-reference match the primary publications and widely used protocol
references as known, and were not individually re-fetched.

### New pathways
- **NATALEE** `natalee` **(fetched)** — Hortobagyi GN et al., *Ann Oncol* 2025;36:149-157
  (doi:10.1016/j.annonc.2024.10.015) and Slamon DJ et al., *Ther Adv Med Oncol* 2023;15
  (doi:10.1177/17588359231178125). Ribociclib 400 mg/day, 3 weeks on / 1 week off, for
  **36 months**, plus a nonsteroidal aromatase inhibitor (letrozole 2.5 mg or anastrozole
  1 mg) for **≥60 months**; goserelin 3.6 mg q28d for men and premenopausal women.
  Population: anatomical stage IIA (N0 with risk factors, or N1), IIB, or III. The app
  shows 3 years of ribociclib + hormone tablet, then 2 further years of the tablet alone
  (156 + 104 weeks = 5 years total). Doses are not shown on the patient page.
  Distinct from the existing `hrplus` pathway, which carries abemaciclib at 2 years
  (monarchE); the two durations differ, which is why this is a separate pathway.
- **Long-course chemoradiation TNT** `tnt-lcrt` **(fetched)** — Fokas E et al.,
  *JAMA Oncol* 2022;8:e215445 (doi:10.1001/jamaoncol.2021.5445), with the pooled
  CAO/ARO/AIO-12 + OPRA analysis, Fokas E et al., *Eur J Cancer* 2024;210:114291
  (doi:10.1016/j.ejca.2024.114291). CRT 50.4 Gy with fluorouracil/oxaliplatin, and
  3 cycles of FOLFOX, in either order; TME scheduled day 123 from the start of TNT.
  The app renders the consolidation order (CRT first) because both trials reported
  higher complete-response rates with CRT→chemotherapy, and the step text states that
  the order may be reversed. The app places surgery at week 19 (day ~126), within the
  protocol window. 3-year DFS was 73% in both arms, so neither order is presented as
  superior for survival.

### Changes to existing pathways
- **KEYNOTE-522** `kn522` — adjuvant capecitabine moved to start after radiation
  finishes, at the owner's direction, and the patient text now says so. **Not
  trial-specified:** KEYNOTE-522 does not include capecitabine, and CREATE-X
  (Masuda, NEJM 2017) specifies only that capecitabine start within 12 weeks of
  surgery, without stating a sequence relative to radiation. This is a
  practice-based ordering, not a protocol requirement. Immunotherapy continues
  throughout, as in the trial.
- **Genomic-assay pathway** `genomic` — dose-dense AC then paclitaxel added as a second
  chemotherapy option alongside docetaxel + cyclophosphamide, switched off by default
  (standard-reference; CALGB 9741 / ECOG 1199, as already cited for the `ddac-t`
  pathway). Modelled as 8 cycles at 14 days (~16 weeks); the text also names the
  weekly-paclitaxel-×12 variant, which the app does not separately time.
- **All surveillance steps (28 across the library)** — now open-ended. Surveillance
  showed fixed totals of 1, 2, 3 or 5 years, which implied an evidence-based endpoint
  that mostly does not exist; the visit frequency is the part guidelines actually
  specify. The map and the step list now read "ongoing" and keep the frequency line,
  which remains editable per patient in the builder. **Note:** the `weeks` value is
  retained on these steps because the layout engine uses it for bar length only; it is
  no longer rendered anywhere and should not be read as a claimed duration.

### Could not confirm
- Whether any guideline specifies capecitabine sequencing relative to radiation after
  neoadjuvant therapy for triple-negative breast cancer. Treated as practice-variable.
- NATALEE overall survival data were still immature at the 2025 final iDFS analysis;
  the pathway makes no survival claim.
