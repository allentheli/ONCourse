Chemotherapy with immunotherapy before surgery, then immunotherapy after. For triple-negative breast cancer, stage II to III.

No one-size-fits-all cancer treatment

Every plan is a sequence of decisions made together: which regimen, in what order, adjusted to the person in front of you. ONCourse makes that sequence visible, so the decision is shared and the road ahead is clear.

🗺️

The whole course on one page

Every phase in order, colored by treatment type, with how often and for how long. Decision points are drawn as real forks, so "it depends on the pathology" becomes something you can see.

✏️

Customized in minutes

Physicians start from a verified regimen, adjust cycles, add or remove steps, mark the expected path, and note why this plan was chosen for this patient. Then print, or hand over a QR code.

🔒

Private by design

There are no accounts and no server. The page runs entirely in the browser, and a share link carries the plan itself, not a record of who it is for.

For patients and families

  • See where you are and what comes next, without decoding a clinic calendar
  • Understand why this plan was chosen for you
  • Keep it on paper, on your phone, or both
  • Tick off steps as you go, and get an updated map at any point in treatment

For physicians

  • Regimens traced to their trials, with sources you can click
  • Edit any step, save your own versions, share a library with colleagues
  • A comparison page for the decision visit: options side by side, on the same time scale
  • Fits one page, US Letter or A4, every time

What's inside

Multimodality plans where sequencing matters most. Each one names its source trial and the date it was last reviewed.

    Built to be trusted

    Every built-in regimen is checked against its primary publication and a structured protocol reference before release, and carries a review date. ONCourse shows the expected plan; the treating team sets the actual dates, which shift with test results and how a person is doing. It is a tool for the conversation, not a substitute for it.
    ONCourse is an educational aid for conversations between patients and their care teams. It does not replace medical advice or your clinic's appointment schedule.
