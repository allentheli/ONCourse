What ONCourse is, and is not

ONCourse is an educational aid. It helps physicians draw a picture of a cancer treatment plan so that it can be explained, discussed, and shared. It is not medical advice, it does not diagnose or recommend treatment for any individual, and it is not a substitute for the judgment of a licensed clinician who knows the patient.

Privacy and data

ONCourse has no user accounts, no server-side storage, and no analytics or tracking. Everything you type runs in your own browser. Two things follow from this design. First, a share link or QR code contains the plan itself, encoded in the link; anyone who has the link can open that plan. For this reason the tool asks you never to enter a patient's name, date of birth, or other identifying information, and we cannot control what a user chooses to type. Second, regimens a clinician saves are stored in that clinician's own browser; clearing browser data removes them, and exporting them is the user's responsibility. Because no information is transmitted to us, ONCourse is not a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA and is not intended to store protected health information.
